Johns Hopkins · Reinforcement Learning

Collision-aware dual policies for agile motion

A simulated Unitree Go2 control system that separates fast, obstacle-aware locomotion from self-righting recovery, with a reach-avoid safety network deciding when to switch. In Isaac Lab, the agile policy reached 2.5 m/s average speed, 4.1 m/s peak speed, 99.60% navigation success, and a 0.34% collision rate. The recovery policy succeeded in 96.85% of triggered cases. Reach–avoid switching reduced collisions from 26.3% to 3.3% and improved goal reaching from 73.3% to 82.0%, with a corresponding timeout trade-off.

Campbell & Company · Research Intern

Crude-oil intelligence

Language signals meet time-series forecasting

Built a domain-specific post-training pipeline for crude-oil news: assembled 12K+ instruction–text–label examples, applied LoRA SFT to ChatGLM2, and fed the resulting sentiment signals into a text–numeric Time-Series Transformer. Four walk-forward folds across 2021–2024 kept the downstream evaluation strictly out of sample.

A 4B-parameter offline medical agent built by post-training Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct with LoRA SFT on approximately 41.9K distilled multimodal trajectories. It is trained to coordinate specialist tools, integrate multimodal evidence, and self-verify its reasoning.
