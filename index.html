04

Johns Hopkins · Reinforcement Learning

Collision-aware dual policies for agile motion

A simulated Unitree Go2 control system that separates fast, obstacle-aware locomotion from self-righting recovery, with a reach-avoid safety network deciding when to switch. The agile policy reached 2.5 m/s average and 4.1 m/s peak speed in Isaac Lab; the full safety stack reduced collisions at the cost of more timeouts and lower end-to-end speed.

03 / PUBLICATION

Compact agents, clinical-scale reasoning.

Research on teaching an offline multimodal model to coordinate tools, retrieve evidence, and verify its own medical reasoning.

Yixiong Chen · Xinyi Bai · Yue Pan · Zongwei Zhou · Alan Yuille

Meissa: Multi-modal Medical Agentic Intelligence

Meissa is a 4B-parameter offline medical agent trained on roughly 40,000 distilled trajectories. It learns to select specialist tools, integrate multimodal evidence, and self-verify—matching or exceeding frontier-scale agents in 10 of 16 evaluation settings while retaining substantially lower inference latency.
